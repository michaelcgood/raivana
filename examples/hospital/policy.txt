Hospital EHR Access Policy

Only doctors can prescribe medication.
Nurses may view patient records during business hours.
Nurses can view any patient record in an emergency.
Physicians may update medical records of patients in their department.
Billing staff can view billing records but cannot view medical records.
Receptionists can create and update appointments.
Psychotherapy notes can only be read by psychiatrists.
Contractors cannot access patient records unless they are on the corporate network.
Nobody may delete audit logs.
All other access is denied.
