Corporate Banking Payments Policy

Tellers can view customer accounts during business hours.
Tellers cannot approve wire transfers.
Managers can approve payments up to $25,000.
Only the CFO may approve payments over $25,000.
Employees cannot approve their own expense reports.
Accountants can read and update the general ledger only from the corporate network.
Auditors can only read financial records.
Contractors must not export payroll data.
Deny by default.
